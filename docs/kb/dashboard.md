## purpose
The Dashboard is the high-level overview and starting point: overall counts,
the latest issues across the platform, and current/upcoming on-duty coverage.
Drill down from here into projects or specific issues.

## layout
- Top row: four stat cards — Total Projects, Open Issues, Resolved Issues, Total
  Issues. Total Projects opens My Projects; the issue cards jump to the issues
  listing focused on that subset (open / resolved / all) when your role has one.
- Recent Issues card: the latest issues; click a row to open the issue detail
  dialog.
- On-Duty Schedule card: current and upcoming Ops duty periods (the current one
  is highlighted).

## flow
1. Read the four stat cards for overall health.
2. Click a stat card to drill into projects or the issues listing.
3. Open a Recent Issue row for its details, or check who's on duty.

## buttons
- **Total Projects** — stat card; opens My Projects.
- **Open Issues** — stat card; opens the issues listing focused on open issues.
- **Resolved Issues** — stat card; opens the issues listing focused on resolved issues.
- **Total Issues** — stat card; opens the full issues listing.
