## purpose
Open Issues is the outstanding-issue board. It lists every OPEN operational
issue you can see and lets the right people claim (self-assign) issues to
themselves — one at a time or in bulk. It is a stable page visible to every
user; what it shows and what you can do depends on your role:
- Global Ops members and admins see every open issue across the whole platform
  and can claim any of them.
- Everyone else sees the open issues of the projects they participate in
  (own / are a member of). They can claim issues only in projects where they
  hold the project-level Ops role; issues in their other projects are view-only.
Handover-review items are not shown here — those live in the Admin Panel.

## layout
- Header "Open Issues" with two count badges: "N open" (issues you can see) and
  "N assigned to you". The subtitle reflects your scope (whole platform, your
  projects, or view-only).
- A search box (filter by id, title, type, project, or assignee).
- When you can claim, an "Assign selected to me" bulk button sits on the right.
- A table with columns: a select checkbox, ID, Issue (title + project/product
  context), Type, Assignee, SLA, Created, and Action.
- Rows you can claim show an enabled checkbox and an "Assign to me" button.
  Rows already assigned to you show "Assigned to you" (green). Rows you may see
  but not claim (a project where you're not a Ops member) show "View only" and
  have no checkbox. Clicking a row opens that issue in the Issue Workbench.

## flow
1. Scan the open issues; use search to narrow by project, type, or assignee.
2. Claim a single issue with its "Assign to me" button, or tick several rows
   (or the header select-all) and use "Assign N to me" to claim them in bulk.
3. Claimed issues become yours — find and work them under My Actions / the
   Issue Workbench (Start Working, record steps, resolve).
4. Rows marked "View only" belong to a project where you're not a Ops member;
   you can see them for awareness but a Ops member (or admin) must claim them.

## buttons
- **Assign to me** — self-assigns that single open issue to you (you become its assignee).
- **Assign selected to me** — bulk-claims every ticked issue to you at once.
