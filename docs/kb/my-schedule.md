## purpose
My Schedule shows your own on-duty assignments — when you are on call and will
receive newly created issues.

## layout
- A current-duty status card at the top: highlighted red ("You are currently on
  duty … until <time>") when you're on call now, otherwise grey ("You are not
  on duty right now") and it lists who is currently on duty.
- A "My Duty Assignments (N)" table with columns Time Range / Role / Status /
  Note. Status is Current / Completed / Upcoming; Role is Primary / Secondary /
  Shadow.

## flow
1. Check the top card to see whether you are on duty right now and until when.
2. Review your upcoming and past duty slots in the assignments table.
